# Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer data from a bank marketing campaign to identify patterns in customer behavior and understand the factors associated with term-deposit subscriptions.

The dashboard was created in Microsoft Excel using data analysis, charts, KPIs, and interactive slicers to present the findings in a clear and easy-to-understand format.

## Dashboard Highlights

- Previous campaign outcomes
- Customer distribution by job and education
- Subscription trends by month
- Housing loan and personal loan analysis
- Marital status comparison
- Campaign contacts vs. deposit subscription
- Average call duration by subscription status
- Overall customer and subscription KPIs

## Key Insights

- 47% of customers subscribed to the deposit campaign.
- Customers with longer call durations showed a higher likelihood of subscribing.
- Subscription performance varied across customer job and education segments.
- Customers without housing loans showed a stronger tendency to subscribe.
- The number of campaign contacts influenced subscription outcomes, highlighting the importance of effective customer communication.
